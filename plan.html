The daily loop

  1. Watch
    Lesson videos at 1.25–1.5×. Press pause whenever you'd write a note.
  2. Learn
    Read the key ideas and the visual. These are short.
  3. Check
    Answer the quick check. Read every explanation, including the ones you got right.
  4. Rate
    Give yourself 1–5 and mark the lesson done. The hub tracks your weak spots.
  5. Break
    Run a 25-minute timer (⏱ in the top bar), then take 5 minutes off. Do 4 rounds, then a long break.
Fell behind? That's normal. Here's the fix.

Don't try to "catch up" by doubling a day. Instead, push the missed lessons into the next light day (Nov 26 or Nov 28). If you miss more than two days, cut the 1.3, 1.8, 2.4 and 3.1 videos to 1.75× and lean on the key ideas. Those objectives are mostly vocabulary.
