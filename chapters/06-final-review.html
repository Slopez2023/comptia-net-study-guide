Avoid brain dumps

Sites selling "real exam questions" break CompTIA's policy and can get your certification revoked.

  1. Take it timed
    90 min, no pausing, phone away
  2. Review every miss
    And every lucky guess
  3. Tag the objective
    Each miss gets an objective number (e.g. 3.4)
  4. Fix the gap
    Rewatch that lesson's video and redo its quick check

Walk in rested, with a plan for the clock.

  • Stop studying by 8 pm. Do nothing new.
  • Get your ID ready and confirm the time. For an online exam, run the Pearson OnVUE system test and clear your desk.
  • Sleep. It's worth more than one more hour of cramming.
  1. Brain dump
    First 3 minutes: write the subnet table, ports and 7 steps
  2. Flag PBQs
    They come first. If one looks long, flag it and move on
  3. MCQs at ~1 min each
    Read the last sentence first. Watch for BEST, FIRST, MOST likely
  4. Back to PBQs
    Use the time you banked. Partial credit is possible
  5. Answer everything
    There's no penalty for guessing
When you're stuck

Eliminate the two obviously wrong answers. Of the two left, pick the one that matches the symptom in the question, not the one that sounds most advanced. On troubleshooting questions, pick the next step in the 7-step method.
